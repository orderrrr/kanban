## Goal

`types.ts` (Ticket, Task, Column), the `api.ts` function signatures and synthetic seed tickets covering every case: each column, overdue, no tasks, all done, at the limits. Settles: dates required or optional, overdue in Done, ticket ID format, browser limits. Done when reviewed with the user.

## Answer

The MVP0 contract in `web/src/lib/`: `types.ts` (tickets moving as a whole between To do, In progress and Done; a schedule of both dates or none; tasks with "t" IDs numbered per ticket and never reused; limits in UTF-8 bytes counted after trimming ASCII whitespace; ID, schedule and overdue rules; nine error codes, the `ApiErrorBody` with 404, 409 and 422, and malformed requests as 400), `api.ts` (the async `Api` interface: every change returns the whole ticket, `setTaskDone` sends the wanted state, drag and drop by `moveTicket(id, column, index)` and `moveTask`, a fixed error order with malformed first, a hidden next task number), and `seed.ts` (seven synthetic tickets, one per case, relative to `today`, with a task-number gap and one ticket at every ticket limit). Components get the `Api` from Svelte context. Six gate rounds; fixes in `d62b44f`, `5aa6362`, `75071a6`, `7b4b95f`, `2a0cc73` and the rewrap.
