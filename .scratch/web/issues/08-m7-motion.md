## Goal

Every change on the board moves instead of jumping: lots of short transitions, asked for by the user on 03/11/2026. Done when the user is happy with it.

## Plan

Rechecked on 03/11/2026 after M6: there are no tasks or card checklists now, so the items about them are dropped and the rest apply to cards and views.

- [x] 1. Motion basics: one `motion.ts` with the few durations used everywhere (short, 120 and 200 ms, eased out), dropped to none when the system asks for reduced motion (or can't be asked, as in tests); CSS transitions use the same values and stop under reduced motion.
- [x] 2. Cards glide: each column's cards and the drop gap become one keyed list, so every card slides to its new place on a drop or a keyboard move.
- [x] 3. Coming and going: a new card unfolds into place, a removed or moved-away one folds away; the drop gap opens and closes rather than appearing.
- [x] 4. Lift and drop: the carried card lifts (slight scale and shadow) as it is picked up; on drop the gap holds until the move is saved, then the card unfolds into it.
- [x] 5. Controls and colour: the pencil and trash icon fade in and out on hover; buttons ease their hover colour; the overdue border fades in.
- [x] 6. Views: opening a ticket, going back up, or back to all tickets slides the new view in; the name and dates editor slides open; messages slide in and out.
- [x] 7. Tests pass, a check in a real browser (with and without reduced motion), and the milestone gate.
- [x] 8. Asked for by the user after the gate: the carried card sways, tilting up to 5 degrees with the pointer's sideways speed and straightening when it rests.
- [x] 9. Asked for by the user after the gate: on release the card glides from the pointer into the gap and settles flat, then takes its place, instead of appearing there at once.

## Answer

Every change moves briefly (120 to 200 ms, eased out) and nothing moves under reduced motion (`motion.ts`, one timing for Svelte and CSS). Cards glide to new places and fade in and out (fading, not folding, so a column keeps its height); the drop gap appears and goes at once; the carried card lifts, sways with the pointer and glides into the gap on drop, where its row appears at once with no flash; hover controls and the overdue border ease; views fly in. Keyboard moves keep focus on the live row. Gate: three rounds plus reviews of the sway, glide and landing fixes (`28bb218`, `b44f53f`, `4d3813b`, `4b7e472`, `64361e4`).
