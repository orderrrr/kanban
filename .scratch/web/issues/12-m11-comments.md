## Answer

Each ticket has comments (plain text, at most 2000 characters, `C-n` IDs never reused, stamped by the `Api`'s clock, which the root passes in). A ticket's view has a "Comments n" toggle that opens a sidebar over the board's right side: a box (Cmd/Ctrl+Enter or an Add button), then the comments of the ticket and every ticket below it, newest first in written order, tagged with their ticket (which opens it, keeping the sidebar open). Removing a ticket removes its comments; old boards load with none. Escape closes the sidebar only when nothing else uses it (carries and boxes hear it first, in the capture phase). `WEB_STYLE.md` 4.3 allows `toISOString()` for a moment from the `Api`'s clock. 324 tests; checked in Chromium and Zen with real input. Gate: five rounds.
